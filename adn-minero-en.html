Why the world needs Chile

The energy transition runs on copper (grids, EVs, renewables) and lithium (batteries). Chile holds the largest copper reserves and vast lithium resources — making it strategic to the global shift to clean energy.

The strategic challenge

Chile leads in producing the resource, but most is exported with little processing. The national strategy is to move up the value chain — cathodes, lithium-to-batteries, green hydrogen and a world-class supplier cluster.
